VibeTradez New feature

You used to see what Claudia picked. Now you can read why.

Every weekday a Claude model makes two calls for you. At 9:25 ET it reads the overnight news and the morning signals and ranks three tickers. At 9:30 ET it reads the live option chain and decides which contracts to actually buy. Until now the only thing you saw was the result.

Starting today you can read the whole thought process. Every quote it pulled, every chain it walked, every order it placed, and the reasoning it wrote along the way is captured and published on the dashboard for each trading day.

What is new

  • Two transcripts per day: the 9:25 selection conversation and the 9:30 execution conversation.
  • Reach them from the dashboard, from any trade page, and from each day in your history.
  • Account balances are redacted. Everything else is the model in its own words.
