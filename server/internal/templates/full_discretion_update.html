Full discretion

One change today, and it is a real one. The model now has full discretion over how the account is allocated. The rule that used to cap how much went into options versus stock is gone. It decides the entire mix on its own from here.

What changed

●  No allocation split

The model can hold all stock, all options, or anything between, and it can put the whole account behind a single name if that is the conviction. Nothing in the code nudges it back toward a target mix anymore.

●  One rule remains, and it is the broker's

The model still spends settled cash only, because a cash account cannot redeploy the proceeds of a sale until they settle the next day. That is broker plumbing, not a risk setting. A buy beyond settled cash is refused, and selling to raise cash is always allowed.

●  More room to be right, more room to be wrong

With the split gone the swings get bigger in both directions. A concentrated options week can run hot or go to zero. The account can lose most or all of its value, and now it can do it faster. That is the show.

The flow from here

Every market day the model reads the book and the tape, sizes its own positions with no allocation limit, and writes down why. The dashboard shows it all live during the day, every trade links to its reasoning, and the recap email still lands after each close. Nothing for you to do except watch.
