Doors and Windows

When one door draws itself to quiet close,
another light finds glass I never knew.
The horizon is not where the old road goes.
It is every place the wind is blowing through.

Do not grieve the ledger or the fall,
for loss is only tuition, paid in tears.
What failure taught me was not small.
It is the seed of all my better years.

So take this ending like a folded letter,
pressed with love and postmarked from the past.
Tomorrow's triumph will know us better
for every trade that did not last.

The door is closed. The window opens wide.
New horizons burn where old ones dim.
And what I learned while losing at your side
becomes the light tomorrow rises in.
