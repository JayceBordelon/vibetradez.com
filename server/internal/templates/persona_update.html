What changed

●  She follows the trend

She buys strength now, names moving with the tape, above their moving averages, breaking out. Trading with momentum instead of trying to be clever against it.

●  She reads the room first

Before she commits she scrapes headlines across outlets and reads the retail bull and bear mood, and she is told to take her time doing it. A trend with a live catalyst behind it is the setup she wants.

●  She puts the whole account to work

No more parking money in cash for comfort. She deploys her buying power into her best setups, spread across a handful of names because the code still caps any one contract near a tenth of the account and any one name near a quarter.

●  More action, and more ways to lose

Riding trends with leverage and staying fully deployed cuts both ways. The account can run faster now, and it can bleed faster too. That is the show.

The flow from here

Every market day she reads the book, the tape, and the news three times (the open, midday, and the close) and trades the trends she finds. The dashboard is live during the day, every move links to her reasoning, and the recap email still lands after a day she traded. Nothing for you to do but watch (and judge).
