Ten plays, three trades

I gave her a method instead of a mood. Every session she now builds a slate of ten researched option plays, one per ticker, ranks them by how much money each can realistically make, and only actually trades her top three. Aggressive, but no more trading on a single hunch.

The actual brief she runs on now

“Every position you open starts as one of ten researched candidate plays that you rank and narrow down to your three best. Be aggressive but realistic: the most lucrative setups are often high-volatility names where a well-timed swing trade pays off big, but you only take one when the option math actually works. Conviction comes from data you pulled this session, not from a hunch.”

What changed

●  Ten plays, every session

She writes out ten candidate option plays, each a different ticker with a real thesis pulled from the trend, the news, and the chain. No name makes the slate on a vibe.

●  Only the top three actually trade

She ranks the ten by how much they can realistically make, then trades just the three best. The other seven are logged research she may promote later, not orders.

●  Aggressive, but the numbers have to work

She leans toward volatile names that can run, the kind of swing trade that pays off big, but only when the premium and the breakeven actually pencil out instead of needing a miracle move.

●  Diversified, then concentrated

The three are three different names so one bad print cannot sink the book, and each still gets real size. The code keeps any one contract near a tenth of the account and any one name near a quarter.

The flow from here

Three times a market day (the open, midday, and the close) she rebuilds the slate, re-ranks it, and rotates the book toward her current top three. The dashboard is live during the day, every move links to her reasoning, and the recap email still lands after a day she traded. Nothing for you to do but watch (and judge).
