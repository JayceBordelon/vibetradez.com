The model picking your trades got smarter. Say hi to Claude Opus 4.8.

Opus 4.7 upgraded to Opus 4.8

Every weekday at 9:25 ET, a Claude model reads the overnight news plus the morning market signals and hands the at-open agent three ranked candidates. At 9:30 the same model reads the live option chain and decides which contracts to actually buy. As of today that model is Anthropic's newest and most capable release, Claude Opus 4.8, taking over from Opus 4.7.

Same flow, same safety caps, same three candidates a day. The brain doing the picking is just sharper. Opus 4.8 reasons better over long context and reads market signals with more nuance, which happens to be the exact part of the job that decides what lands in your morning email. The silly little model is now a slightly less silly little model.

What changed

  • Both the pre-bell picker (9:25 ET) and the at-open agent (9:30 ET) now run on Claude Opus 4.8.
  • Every hard cap is unchanged. Still three candidates a day, still a $10 per share limit on any contract the agent fires.
  • Nothing for you to do. Your next morning email is already powered by the new model.
