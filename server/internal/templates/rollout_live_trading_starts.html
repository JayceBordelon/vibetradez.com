VibeTradez ⚠ GOING LIVE

Tomorrow morning, trades go LIVE.

Up until now, every trade you saw VibeTradez take was paper — Claude picked it, the dashboard tracked it, but no actual order ever hit a broker. Starting at the 9:30 AM ET open, that flips. The rank-1 pick of the day will be a real Schwab order, with my real money on the line.

You don't have to do anything. The dashboard, the morning email, the EOD summary — they all keep working exactly the same. The only thing that changes is the badge on the trade card flips from PAPER to LIVE.

● Holding @ $1.85 PAPER What you saw before today.
● Holding @ $1.85 LIVE What you'll see starting tomorrow.

The brakes are still on

Live mode has the same hard guardrails as paper mode had. Nothing about the rules changed; the only thing that changed is whether the order is real:

1. One contract per day, max. Hardcoded. Even if a future bug computed N>1, the order builder panics.
2. Hard cap: $500 per trade. If the contract premium is above $5/share at pick time, no order. Worst-case daily loss is bounded.
3. Mandatory close at 3:55 PM ET. Every position is unconditionally closed five minutes before market close. No overnight risk.
4. Rank-1 only. Ranks 2-10 are still surfaced for tracking but never get a real order. Only the top pick gets fired.

Why now

I've been running paper mode for a while to build trust in the system end-to-end: Claude's pick lands, the order constructs, the close cron fires, the EOD email matches the dashboard, the granular health endpoint stays green. It does. Time to put a buck on it. I want skin in the game so the picks actually matter to me too — not just to the score sheet.

Where to watch the show

  • Dashboard. The trade card on the rank-1 pick will show the LIVE badge and the live entry / current option mark.
  • History. Each day's breakdown shows the same LIVE badge plus realized P&L on close.
  • Trade detail. Click any contract to see Claude's rationale alongside the actual entry / exit / realized P&L.
