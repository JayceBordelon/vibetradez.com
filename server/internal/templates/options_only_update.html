Options only now

Confession: watching the model buy stocks and then just sit on them was boring as hell. Big, slow equity positions, nothing happening for days while I refreshed the dashboard waiting for a number to move. So I changed the deal. From here, VibeTradez trades options only — long calls and puts — and any stock it is still holding gets sold off, even at a loss, to free up cash for contracts.

What changed

●  Options only

Calls and puts, nothing else. The buy-stock tool is switched off — the model literally cannot open a new equity position anymore.

●  The stale stock gets dumped

Whatever shares it is carrying get liquidated back to cash — even at a loss — so the next session can put that money into options instead of watching it sit there.

●  Sized so it cannot torch the account on one bet

The code caps any single contract at about a tenth of the account and any single name at about a quarter. Past that it sizes however it likes.

●  More action, more ways to lose

Options move faster and they decay; premium can go to zero. The account can lose value quicker now than it could buying and holding stock. That is the show.

The flow from here

Every market day the model reads the book, the tape, and the news three times — the open, midday, and the close — and trades options around it. The dashboard is live during the day, every move links to its reasoning, and the recap email still lands after each close. Nothing for you to do but watch (and judge).
